"It's not the screen time that's frying our kids' brains. It's the loop."

**[INSIGHT, 20–30s]** *(Text on screen: JAMA 2025)*
"Then I read a 2025 study of 4,000 kids. Total screen time didn't predict the mental-health problems. Addictive patterns did. The loop is the problem."

**[SOLUTION, 30–60s]** *(Fast cuts of the real app screens)*
"So I built Tube Timer.
Science, space, math, music: always allowed.
Brain-rot? A small daily allowance, and he has to choose it on purpose." *(Hold up screen)*
"Try something off-limits?" *(Ah ah ah character)* "Ah ah ah. Here are five better videos."
"And when time's up…" *(dance party, chickens)* "…the screen makes him get up and move."
"Want more time? Earn it. Watch a math video, answer five questions." *(Earn screen)*

"And here's what I didn't expect: when the loop breaks, he gets up. He plays piano. He goes outside. He's still in there."
